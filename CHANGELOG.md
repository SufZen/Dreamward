## 0.4.0 — first public release

### Your book
- **12 life areas.** In each, write what you believe, the life you envision, who you are becoming, why it matters and how you will get there. Plus a cover, a *dream life* page of guided questions, and implementation pages. Bilingual (English/Hebrew, full RTL). The wording comes from a replaceable **framework pack** (`docs/framework-packs.md`).
- **Current Life Chapter.** Name the season you're in and choose up to five focus areas. Mark the areas that only need maintenance, and keep a *not now* list and a *no longer acceptable* list.
- **Life wheel.** A 1–10 rating per area ("how close is today to my vision?"), kept as append-only history and shown as a radar on the dashboard, with your biggest gaps.
- **IKIGAI.** A guided wizard that walks through the four circles, maps their overlaps and ends with your statement and a first step. Earlier versions are kept.
- **Goals, actions, journal, vision boards and snapshots.** Progress, momentum and at-risk signals, full-text search, a collage editor with multi-format export, and then-vs-now comparison.

### Lify, on your own AI
- An AI companion that knows your book: streaming chat, proposals you approve, and scheduled routines (daily plan, weekly review prep, goal drift).
- **Bring your own AI**: 13 provider presets (OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, OpenRouter, Groq, Together, DeepSeek, Mistral, Ollama, LM Studio, Lemonade, LiteLLM and a custom endpoint). Includes model discovery and a per-user *My AI usage* view. The server never pays for AI.

### Your agents
- **MCP server**: 37 annotated tools (read keys only see the read tools), resources, and Lify's rituals as prompts. Your Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini subscription can run the coaching.
- **`dreamward setup <agent>`** connects Claude Code or Desktop, Cursor, VS Code, Windsurf, Gemini CLI, Codex or OpenCode with one command. `dreamward mcp` runs the server.
- REST `/api/v1` with personal API keys and an audit trail. OpenAPI 3.1 at `/api/v1/openapi.json`, `/api/v1/docs`. Also `AGENTS.md`, `llms.txt` and a portable agent skill.

### Run it your way
- **Desktop app** for Windows, macOS and Linux: no server and no login. The book lives in your app-data folder, secrets sit in the OS keychain, and updates take a backup first.
- **Self-hosted**:
  - Hardened multi-arch images and `deploy/` compose (automatic HTTPS, or local/LAN).
  - `install.sh`, plus `dreamward-ctl` with automatic rollback.
  - First-run setup in the browser, invites and an admin dashboard.

### Your data is safe
- Every account has its own database.
- Every upgrade is integrity-checked and snapshotted first, and a downgrade guard refuses data written by a newer version.
- Daily verified backups. Download your whole book and import it anywhere.
- SSRF protection for AI provider URLs, including redirects.
