Install Dreamward.

Free, and yours: your book stays on your computer, and new versions install themselves.

Dreamward runs on computers (Windows, Mac, Linux). Open dreamward.life on your computer to download it.

Download for Windows Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
  1. Open the file you downloadedDreamward-Setup.exe, in your Downloads folder.
  2. If you see "Windows protected your PC"Click More info, then Run anyway. It shows once because early versions aren't signed yet.
  3. Follow the installerDreamward opens when it's done. Updates install themselves.
Download for Mac (Apple silicon) Download for Mac (Intel)

Not sure which? Apple menu → About This Mac. If it says Chip: Apple M…, choose Apple silicon. If it says Processor: Intel, choose Intel.

  1. Open the downloaded fileDrag Dreamward into your Applications folder.
  2. Open it the first time with a right-clickIn Applications, right-click (or Control-click) Dreamward, choose Open, then Open again. It's needed once because early versions aren't signed yet.
  3. If macOS says the app "is damaged"Open Terminal, run xattr -cr /Applications/Dreamward.app, then open Dreamward again.
Download AppImage (any Linux) Download .deb (Ubuntu, Debian) 64-bit (x86_64)
  1. AppImage: make it runnableRight-click the file, open Properties and allow running it as a program. Or run chmod +x Dreamward-*.AppImage.
  2. Double-click to startDreamward opens. Updates install themselves.
  3. Prefer the .deb?Run sudo apt install ./Dreamward-*.deb in the folder you downloaded it to.

How it works

Small steps toward a north star.

Plans go stale when they live in a slide deck. Dreamward keeps your vision next to what you do this week, so the two stay connected.

See it

A book of your life in twelve areas, from health and love to money and meaning.

  • What you believe and what you envision
  • Who you are becoming, and why it matters
  • Your dream life, question by question

Choose it

Not everything matters equally in every season. Name the chapter you're in.

  • Up to five focus areas, and a not now list
  • A 1–10 life wheel: how close is today to the vision?
  • IKIGAI: find your reason to get up in the morning

Walk it

Turn the vision into goals and actions you can do this week.

  • Goals with honest progress and at-risk signals
  • Small actions, a journal and vision boards
  • A weekly review that keeps you on the path
