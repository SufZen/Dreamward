# Code of Conduct

This project is about helping people grow. We want the community around it to
feel the same way: warm, honest and safe — for every age, body, background,
belief, gender identity, orientation, nationality, language, level of
experience and ability.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that builds this community:

- being kind, patient and generous with your knowledge
- respecting different life paths, values and views — this is a tool for
  *your* vision, not ours
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- taking responsibility, apologising, and learning from mistakes
- protecting other people's privacy — never share anyone's personal content
  (journals, visions, screenshots of someone's book) without consent

Unacceptable behaviour includes:

- harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks
- sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention
- publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- proselytising, or pushing a belief system on others
- any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, discussions, pull requests, chat,
events) and when representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the maintainers (see the maintainer contact in
[MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md), or open a private GitHub security advisory
if nothing else is available). All reports are reviewed promptly and kept
confidential. Maintainers may, in proportion to the harm: ask for correction,
issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban.
